## Step-by-Step: How to Get Help

### Step 1 — Start a Conversation

Open WhatsApp and send **any message** (e.g., "Hi", "Hello", "Help") to:

> 📱 **+91 94009 61291**

The AI bot will respond immediately with a welcome menu:

```
👋 Welcome to Poornasree Equipments!

Please choose an option:
1️⃣ Troubleshoot my equipment
2️⃣ View our products
3️⃣ Speak to support
4️⃣ Track my service request
```

---

### Step 2 — Register / Verify Your Machine

If you select **Troubleshoot**, the bot will first ask for your **machine serial number** to verify your equipment.

**How to find your serial number:**
- Check the **sticker on the back or bottom** of the machine
- Check your **purchase invoice**
- Check the **warranty card**

**Example interaction:**
```
Bot: Please enter your machine serial number.

You: 251052614693

Bot: ✅ Machine Verified!
     Machine: Lactosure Pro 2.0
     Customer: John Doe
     Invoice Date: 15-Jan-2025
     Warranty: 24 months (Valid)
```

> **Note**: If your serial number is not found in our system, the bot will ask you to contact support directly.

---

### Step 3 — Self-Service Troubleshooting

Once your machine is verified, the bot presents a **list of common issues** to choose from:

```
What problem are you facing?

1️⃣ Machine not powering on
2️⃣ No display / blank screen
3️⃣ Inaccurate readings
4️⃣ Connectivity issues (Wi-Fi / Cloud)
5️⃣ Alarm / error beeping
6️⃣ Other (describe in your own words)
```

#### For known issues (Options 1–5):
The bot walks you through **step-by-step troubleshooting**:

```
Bot: Step 1 — Check that the power cable is firmly connected to the wall socket and the machine.

     Did this resolve the issue?
     ✅ Yes, resolved!
     ❌ No, still having the issue
```

Continue following the steps. Many issues can be resolved this way without needing a field engineer.

#### For "Other" issues (Option 6):
Type your problem in your own words. The AI will understand your description and try to guide you.

```
You: The machine shows E4 error and makes a beeping sound every 5 minutes

Bot: I understand you're seeing an E4 error. Let me help you with that...
```

### Step 5 — Raising a Service Ticket

If troubleshooting doesn't resolve your problem, the bot will offer to raise a **service ticket** for you:

```
Bot: I'm sorry we couldn't resolve this remotely. Would you like us to send a service engineer?

1️⃣ Yes, raise a service request
2️⃣ No, I'll try later
```

If you select **Yes**, the bot will collect:

| Information Needed | Why |
|---|---|
| Your **Name** | To address you properly |
| Your **Location / City** | To find the nearest engineer |
| Your **Pincode** | For precise geographic routing |
| **Describe the problem** | To brief the engineer before arrival |

**Example:**
```
Bot: What is your name?
You: Raju Kumar

Bot: What is your city and pincode?
You: Chennai, 600001

Bot: Please briefly describe the issue.
You: Machine shows E4 error and shuts off after 30 minutes
```

Once submitted, your ticket is created:
```
Bot: ✅ Service request raised!
     Ticket No: TKT-20260722-004
     We'll assign an engineer and notify you on WhatsApp.
```

---

### Step 6 — Receiving Updates

After your ticket is raised, you will receive **automatic WhatsApp updates**:

```
📢 Update on Ticket TKT-20260722-004:
An engineer has been assigned to your request.
👨‍🔧 Engineer: Suresh Kumar
📅 Expected Visit: 24 July 2026
```

---

### Step 7 — Engineer Arrives & OTP Verification

When the engineer arrives at your location and completes the repair, they will ask for a **4-digit OTP (One-Time Password)** to officially close the ticket.

**How it works:**
1. The engineer requests OTP closure from the system
2. You receive a WhatsApp message with the OTP:

```
Bot: 🔐 Your OTP to confirm engineer visit:

     OTP: 7453

     Please share this with the engineer on-site.
     This OTP is valid for 30 minutes.
```

3. Share the OTP with the engineer
4. The engineer enters it in the system — the ticket is officially **CLOSED**

> ⚠️ **Never share your OTP before the engineer arrives or completes the repair.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Do I need to install an app?
**A:** No. Everything works through WhatsApp. Just save **+91 94009 61291** and message us.

### Q: How do I know my ticket status?
**A:** You will receive WhatsApp notifications at each stage. You can also message us with your ticket number to check.

### Q: What if I don't know my machine serial number?
**A:** Check the sticker on your machine or your purchase invoice. If still not found, type "Help" and request to speak with support.

### Q: What if the OTP expires?
**A:** If the OTP expires (after 30 minutes), the engineer can request a new one. A fresh OTP will be sent to your WhatsApp.

### Q: Is my data safe?
**A:** Yes. Your data is used only to manage your service request. Poornasree does not share your information with third parties.

### Q: What hours is support available?
**A:** The AI bot is available **24/7**. Human support agents are available during business hours. For urgent matters outside business hours, the AI will record your request for follow-up.

- ✅ Keep WhatsApp notifications turned on so you don't miss updates
